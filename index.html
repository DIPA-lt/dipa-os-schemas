DIPA OS · schemas · 21 Oct agreement, phone updated 22 Oct

Each route exists to produce one result.

A lead is not a client. Cash is not a sale. A sent invoice is not a paid invoice. A step that does not change the stock is not a stage. Lithuanian words are the words on the screen.

01

The desk. One company, from arrival to a deal.

This is the seller’s route on the lead. It is not the dashboard funnel. A person confirms before anything is written. Discard writes nothing.

1

A company becomes a lead Works today

Website form · Kajabi · partner sheet (KROT, Meta) · Lead Finder · one company picked from Monday · typed or imported

Every door writes the same lead record. The source is kept. Lead Finder goes search, then ICP score A/B/C/D, then a quick screen, then deep research, then promote.

ResultOne desk. Owner, phone, email, company. The same business does not need a second kind of record.

2

Monday check, before anyone works it Works today

Read-only lookup · cached 7 days · recheck on demand

Nėra CRM: nothing in Monday, work it as new. Jau CRM: the company exists, confirm before assigning. Užimta: active client or another seller’s open deal, do not assign. Nepatikrinta: lookup failed. A failed lookup is never shown as clear.

ResultThe seller knows whether to start, confirm, or stop. A duplicate is not created by accident.

3

The lead is the desk Works today

Lauris opens Leads. Three tabs, three jobs.

Šiandien is the action: owner, Warm/Cold set only by a person, the phone, the deal, the latest call, Siųsti į Monday. Prieš skambutį is the brief: who to call, with evidence, up to three builds, three implementations, three opening questions. Empty means research has not been run. Medžiaga is the file: score, tier, maturity, background.

ResultMonday morning starts on the lead, not on a dashboard. What to do, what to read, and the file are three places on purpose.

4

Call from the lead Works today

The button on the lead is the habit. The floating phone is the second choice.

Dial the number on this lead. The pre-call card shows who matched, then Call now. Coach and transcript sit beside the call. Nesutiko ends the call and drops the transcript. Call history gets a row either way. A short call gets no draft. Outbound from +370 647 21374 was tested on 22 Oct and goes through. An inbound call onto the right lead has not been shown.

ResultThe attempt is visible on the lead, whether or not they talked. The team does not keep a second log of the same call.

5

Confirm writes. Discard writes nothing. Works today

The seller. The model may draft. It does not write alone.

Confirm stores the pipeline stage, Warm or Cold only if the seller chose one, a follow-up task “Susisiekti: {company}” on the owner, and a calendar event only when date and time are both set. Email and LinkedIn text stay for the seller to copy. Confirm does not send them. If the lead was already pushed to Monday, the review updates Monday in the background.

ResultThe next morning has an owner and a task. A discarded draft leaves the lead as it was.

6

The stage, in Monday’s words Works today

Set by the seller. Nedomina can be set from any stage.

Bendravimas atidėtas → Deriname susitikimą → Suderintas susitikimas → Laukia pasiūlymo → Laukiame sprendimo → Laukiame apmokėjimo → Klientas (sumokėjo). This pipe is the desk. It is not the six-stage dashboard funnel.

ResultDIPA OS and Monday use the same status words, so a push does not translate the lead into a second language.

7

Money is a deal, born on the lead Works today

Atidaryti sandorį on Šiandien. One open deal per lead. There is no create button on the Deals page.

Didžiosios dirbtuvės store seats and a month, a place in the room of 40. Dedikuotos store hours and a month, Mantas’s time, not a seat. Prenumerata, PLAUD, and Kita store a title and an amount. Mark won and Mark lost are a person’s click. A sent invoice does not flip the deal. Deleting the deal keeps a workshop enrollment already attached.

ResultThe money object belongs to that company. A seat, a block of hours, and a subscription are not the same number.

8

The letter is drafted on the lead Works today

The seller sends it. The app does not.

Pradžia for a first or cold letter. Tęsinys to remind or return after a pause. Pokalbis to invite, confirm a time, or follow a call. Sprendimas for the proposal, the wait, payment, a check-in, or leaving the door open.

ResultThe letter matches the stage the lead is actually in. Sending stays a human act.

9

Monday, once, then kept in step Works today

Siųsti į Monday pushes onto the Marketing Leads board. The Leads board inside the DIPA CRM workspace is a different board.

One push. If the company is already known, the seller confirms before a second item. After that the button becomes Atidaryti Monday. Later confirmed reviews update summary, stage, follow-up date, and contact date. A lead that was never pushed stays only in DIPA OS.

ResultMarketing has one item per company, and later changes do not require a second copy.

02

Before a lead exists, the message is sorted.

The route exists so Lauris’s time is qualification and proposals. Delivery, repeats, and tests do not enter his queue.

Test of the virtual operator Agreed

The message is labelled TESTAS. The virtual operator is a pilot, not a seller.

ResultIt is not a lead. It does not enter the warehouse and it is not counted as demand.

Repeat, delivery, or “something doesn’t work” Not built

This belongs to Aušrinė. Today it can still land on Lauris. He marks Report a problem once and forwards the contact. After a few reports the system is supposed to route the pattern itself.

ResultAušrinė’s bank holds repeats and delivery. Lauris is not the inbox for work that is not a sale.

Otherwise it is a lead Agreed

Warm: a person wrote first, from the web, ads, or LinkedIn. Cold: Lead Finder, DIPA found them. The two qualities are never added into one number.

ResultThe warehouse starts at zero steps taken by DIPA. Warm demand and cold prospecting stay visible as two stocks.

03

The agreed funnel. A stage’s result is the next stock.

Draw this for Pardavimai, not for the lead card. The screen at /os/v2 is a first cut and is looser than this agreement. Igoris still confirms the funnel with Lauris and Mantas. Open

sėdi = buvo + atėjo − išėjo. Δ = sėdi now − sėdi a week ago. The same number is not drawn twice.

1 · System

Sandėlis

Raw material. Lauris has not picked it up. Arrivals split into warm and Lead Finder. Nobody is called from here.

ResultEveryone leaves only into pre-qualification. The untouched pile is visible.

2 · System

Prikvalifikacija

Automatic. Not Lauris and not a phone call. Unscored is neither AB nor CD.

ResultThree stocks: AB bank, CD bank, and unscored. The call has not happened yet.

3 · Lauris

Kvalifikacinis skambutis

His week is how many calls he made. The number is written by the call, not by a second CRM entry. Changing AB to CD after the call is agreed and not in the app. Not built

Pralaimėta. Not interested. This is lost, not relabelled as D.
Liko banke. Still sitting.
Nuėjo į pasiūlymą.

ResultThe call has exactly three exits. Lost is a loss. Sitting stays a stock. The rest become proposals.

4 · Lauris

Pasiūlymas

Sending the proposal, and the proposal session. Norm is 5 days from the lead to here. A client can change quantity after the proposal. From here Lauris has less influence.

ResultCount sent, sum ex VAT, and the open bank. The open bank is the forecast. There is no separate forecast page.

5 · Client

Sprendimas

The client said yes this week. A decision can still be pushed, but the stage is the yes.

ResultA count of yeses. Not euros. Money stays on Pinigai.

6 · Won

Won

The meeting closed on invoice sent, plus a count of cancelled invoices. Lauris does not accept “invoice sent” as won. He wants paid to mean won. Open Paid stays on Pinigai either way. People who paid this week are not the people who were won this week, because payment lags.

ResultA sale is an issued invoice. Cash collected is a different box. A cancelled invoice is fallout, not a silent disappearance.

04

Three sheets. They do not share the same numbers.

First level is the picture. A click goes deeper. Pardavimai, invoices issued, are ex VAT. Pajamos, cash that landed, are with VAT. Skola is with VAT, split into vėluoja and nevėluoja.

Pinigai Agreed

Igoris and the weekly picture. Sales ex VAT, cash with VAT, debt in two numbers, then the same sales sum by product (didžiosios, dedikuotos, kitos) and by seller. Workshop seats by month name. Plan versus fact. A red lamp when the week is under break-even needs one number they still have to hand. Not built Aušrinė types the invoice until Business Central sends “paid”. Not built

ResultThe room can see whether the week is making money, without opening the funnel.

Pardavimai Agreed

Lauris. The funnel above, then speed: days to first touch, days to proposal against a 5-day norm, minutes per client from the calls. A bottleneck table names who is sitting and the age in days. Red at 14 days. The next action is the one that moves the most money. Quality comes from the call, not from typing the same facts into a second CRM. Off this page: cash, debt, VAT.

ResultLauris sees what is stuck and spends the week on the pile that frees the most money. His remaining work is the call and the proposal.

Strategija Agreed

The third tab is labelled CEO. The warehouse, and whether it grew. AB bank and CD bank, with Δ. Unscored in neither. Source mix, warm separate from cold. The proposal bank, open count and sum ex VAT, is the forecast. Average sold per client, the example was 2 or 2.5 seats, resizes how many people must sit at each stage. One money line only, so this page is not a second Pinigai. Seller capacity needs a month of data. Not built

ResultOwners see whether the warehouse can feed the plan, and how many must sit at each stage to hit it.

The week plan Not built

Five windows: next week’s plan, last week’s plan, last week’s fact, a comment, a recommendation. The plan names calls, proposals, and decisions. It does not name a euro target. The comment says what was planned badly. It becomes dynamic from fact only after a month of Lauris’s weeks. Spoken example for week 42, not live data: next week 3 calls, 10 proposals, 3 decisions.

ResultNext week’s work is named from last week’s fact, and the five largest clients are pushed first inside the 5-day norm.

What the route refuses to mix

The sales page does not show euros. The money page does not show the funnel. No separate forecast. The headline is the sum of the people, not one person’s number. A case that arrives to Mantas normally goes to Lauris, unless the client asks Mantas to finish it.

ResultEach owner opens one sheet and gets one question answered. The same fact is not maintained in two places.

05

Where a number lands. Three counts, not one sale.

Do not draw one box called “the sale” that writes all of these.

Workshop seats Works today

A seat counts toward the 40 only when it is invoiced or paid, the amount is above zero, and it is not a repeat and not a trial. A month drops off the strip once its session has ended.

ResultThe room of 40 shows paid places. Repeaters, trial, and free stay on the list and do not fill a seat.

My week Works today

Mantas’s hours are typed on My week. Mantas time only reads them.

ResultDedicated-workshop capacity is hours, not seats. The board cannot drift from what was typed.

Sales OS v2 invoices Works today

Aušrinė types them on Pinigai. This ledger is what the dashboard reads. It is not the lead desk. Business Central is meant to take “paid” over. Not built

ResultThe weekly money picture has one ledger. Until Business Central is connected, that ledger is what Aušrinė types.

Tasks and calendar Works today

A confirmed call creates the follow-up. A meeting is created only when date and time are both set. A practice call must not create a real seller task. Not built on the local practice path.

ResultThe seller returns to Šiandien on the due date. The next step is not a note that lives only in the call.
