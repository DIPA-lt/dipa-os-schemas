DIPA OS · agreed schemas · 21 Oct 2026 · redrawn 26 Oct

Each route states the result it exists to produce.

These are the mermaid schemas of the sales process and the three sheets. A lead is not a client. Cash is not a sale. A sent invoice is not a paid invoice. Lithuanian words are the words on the screen. The desk pipe and the six-stage funnel are two different routes.

A stage is a stockArrived, left, still sitting, and the week change. Sitting = was + arrived − left.
The result is the next stockA stage that only feels warmer is not a stage. A refusal is lost.
One number, one placeThe first level is the picture. A click goes deeper. The same fact is not drawn twice.
VATPardavimai, invoices issued, are ex VAT. Pajamos and skola are with VAT.

1

Three sheets. Three questions.

Pinigai, Pardavimai, and Strategija do not share the same numbers. Only one figure travels between them. The screen’s third tab is labelled CEO.

Result of this routeEach owner opens one sheet and gets one question. Euros stay on Pinigai. The funnel stays on Pardavimai. The warehouse stays on Strategija.

2

Vartai. Before it is a lead.

Lauris receives sales only. A test, a repeat course, a delivery question, and a broken thing are sorted before the warehouse. The web form already lands in DIPA OS. The virtual operator is a pilot, not a seller.

Result of this routeThe sales warehouse starts clean. Lauris’s time is left for the qualification call and the proposal.

3

Piltuvėlis. Six stages. The result is the next stock.

Every arrow is a movement the system can count: it left one stage and arrived in the next. Sitting = was + arrived − left. The week change is sitting now minus sitting a week ago. This is the agreed funnel. It is not the eight statuses on the lead card.

Result of this routeThe week can be read as stocks. A refusal is a loss, an open proposal is the forecast, and cash is collected on a different sheet.

Still open with Lauris and Mantas. The drawing is the agreement from 19 Oct, and Igoris confirms it with them before it is final. Lauris today also calls C leads. This drawing treats AB as the working bank and CD as the other stock.
4

One worked week, in Igoris’s numbers.

An example he spoke, not this week’s data. He also said 110 and 120 while revising. The set below is the one that balances. He did not split the last 3, so the schema does not invent that split. The 2 without a letter stay unscored.

Result of this routeA week can be checked. Sitting is arithmetic, and a missing split stays missing.

5

Pardavimai. Does the funnel move?

Lauris’s sheet. Counts and days. No euros. He does not type the number by hand. It comes from the call and from the stage change. His green zone is the qualification call and the proposal.

6

How the week plan is born.

Five windows: next week’s plan, last week’s plan, last week’s fact, a comment, a recommendation. The plan names calls, proposals, decisions, and clients. It does not name a euro target. It becomes dynamic from last week’s fact only after a month of Lauris’s weeks. That loop is not built yet. The spoken example for week 42, not live data: next week 3 qualification calls, 10 proposals, 3 decisions. The fact he gave for the week before: 2 qualification calls, 9 proposals sent, 7 deals covering 10 seats. “Prikvalifikacijos du” here means his calls, not the automatic split.

7

Pinigai. Are we making money?

The headline is always the sum of the people. Aušrinė enters the amount and who sold it, until Business Central does. The seller is paid from that name, so the name on the invoice is required. She marks it paid by hand until Business Central sends that. The break-even lamp needs one number they still have to hand. There is no cost table on this sheet.

What this sheet refusesNo funnel, no call counts, no ROI sessions, no second forecast, no second “invoices with VAT” line, no action list, no lead counts.

8

Strategija. Will raw material and people be enough?

Igoris and Mantas. The sheet counts backward from the income plan. The multiplier is not a second finance page. One money line, and that is all. A case that arrives to Mantas normally goes to Lauris. The exception is a client who asks Mantas to finish it. Warm and cold are never added into one quality number.

9

The desk. One company, from arrival to the next morning.

This is the route on the lead, and it works today. It is not the six-stage funnel. A person confirms before anything is written. Discard writes nothing. Outbound from +370 647 21374 was tested on 22 Oct and goes through. An inbound call landing on the right lead has not been shown.

Still not on this routeA practice call must not create a real seller task or a calendar event. Changing AB to CD after the call is a funnel rule, and the desk cannot do it yet.

10

Where a number lands.

Four counts leave the same week. They are different objects. One box called “the sale” does not write all of them.

Result of this routeA seat, an hour, an invoice, and a task can each be true without forcing the other three.

Open, on purpose.

  3. Business Central filling invoices and paid status. Aušrinė types both until then.
  4. The break-even number, the seller-capacity signal, and the week plan that rewrites itself. Each needs data the sheet does not have yet.
